How SubletWorks Works

1

Float a Tender

PM creates a BOQ, sets payment terms & retention, publishes to matching contractors by area & trade.

2

Receive & Compare Bids

Contractors bid item-wise or lump sum. PM sees L1/L2/L3 ranking instantly and requests revisions.

3

Accept & Unlock Contact

Once both sides accept, a one-time ₹99 unlock reveals phone, chat & email for that project.

4

Execute & Bill

Work Order, Gantt/Kanban planning, DPRs, Hindrance requests, MB Sheet and RA Bills — all connected.
