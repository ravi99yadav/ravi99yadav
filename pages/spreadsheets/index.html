❔ Supported formulas & tips

Start a formula with =. Reference cells like A1, ranges like A1:A10. Operators: + - * / ^ and % (e.g. =A1*18%).

Functions: SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, COUNTA, PRODUCT, ROUND, ROUNDUP, ROUNDDOWN, INT, ABS, SQRT, POWER, MOD, IF, AND, OR, NOT, LEN, CONCAT.

Examples: =SUM(B2:B20) · =A1*C1 · =ROUND(D5*1.18, 2) · =IF(E2>100000, "High", "Normal") · =AVERAGE(F2:F10)
